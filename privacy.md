# Stubborn Balls — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

Stubborn Balls is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases, and it never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

## What the game collects

**Game progress** (which levels you have cleared, your daily-board streak) is stored only on your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

**Anonymous usage data.** The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how far players get, so levels can be tuned. It records events such as "level started", "level completed" and "level failed" with the level number, together with standard technical data Firebase collects automatically:

- an app-instance identifier generated by Firebase (it can be reset by reinstalling the app)
- device model, operating-system version, language and country-level region
- app version, session length and first-open time

This data is not linked to your name or contact details, is not used for advertising, and is not sold. Google processes it on our behalf under its own terms: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## The website's beta sign-up

If you join the beta through our website, we keep the first name and email address you enter, the time, and which page the form was on. They are stored in a private Google Sheet owned by the developer and are used only to run the beta and to tell you about the launch. They are not shared or sold. Ask for removal at any time using the contact address on the game's store page, and your entry will be deleted.

## What the game does not do

- No advertising identifiers are used and no ads are shown.
- No data is shared with advertisers or data brokers.
- The motion sensor, camera, microphone and location are not used.

## Children

Stubborn Balls is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Your choices

You can stop all collection by deleting the app. To ask for analytics data tied to your app instance to be deleted, contact the developer using the contact address shown on the game's App Store or Google Play page.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
